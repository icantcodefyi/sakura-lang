# Vow brand: Sakura Seal

Ink on paper, one sakura accent. The mark is the seal the compiler presses on a signature it has
checked: five notched petals around a magenta core.

Assets live in [`public/brand/`](../public/brand) and are generated by
[`sakura-seal.py`](sakura-seal.py) (`python3 design/sakura-seal.py` from `website/`; the lockup
needs `fonttools`). Edit the parameters there, never the SVGs by hand.
[`logo-sakura-seal-draft.svg`](logo-sakura-seal-draft.svg) is the approved sketch it refines.

## Palette

| Token      | Hex       | Role                                      | On paper | On ink |
| ---------- | --------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------- | ------ |
| Ink        | `#1E1E1E` | text, primary buttons, dark background    | 16.6:1   | n/a    |
| Paper      | `#FEFEFE` | background, text on ink                   | n/a      | 16.6:1 |
| Line       | `#DEDEDE` | hairlines, borders                        | n/a      | n/a    |
| Line 2     | `#C4C4C4` | strong borders, inputs                    | n/a      | n/a    |
| Sakura     | `#F386A1` | the accent: petals, fills, large marks    | 2.4:1    | 6.9:1  |
| Sakura ink | `#B4245F` | sakura as text on light (links, labels)   | 6.2:1    | n/a    |
| Magenta    | `#D45BB6` | the core; deep accent, used sparingly     | 3.5:1    | 4.7:1  |
| Sky        | `#9FC3E7` | cool counterpoint in gradients only       | 1.8:1    | 9.1:1  |
| Blush      | `#FCE7EE` | washes behind highlighted rows and panels | n/a      | n/a    |

Sakura fails 4.5:1 on paper, so in light mode it never carries small text: use ink for text,
sakura for marks and fills, and sakura ink for links. In dark mode sakura is readable as text.
Code colours are tuned separately in `lib/vow/themes.ts` (every token ≥ 4.5:1 on its code
background).

Proportion: paper and ink do almost all the work. Sakura appears in the mark, one primary moment
per screen, and washes; magenta only in the core and the deepest point of a gradient; sky only in
the hero clouds.

## Mark construction

Unit: the mark's 100 × 100 box.

1. **Wedges.** Each petal owns a 72° wedge around the centre.
2. **Gaps are cut out.** A petal's straight sides are its wedge edges moved inward by half the gap
   `g`, so neighbours are separated by a parallel channel of width `g`. The petal base is an arc of
   radius `core + g`, concentric with the core, so the ring between core and petals is also `g`.
   Nothing is painted in the background colour: the mark works on any background and in one colour.
3. **Silhouette.** From the side the outline leaves the channel, swells to its widest point at about
   70% of the radius, rounds over a lobe and drops into a shallow V notch. Three cubics, mirrored.
4. **Optical centre.** The upward petal reaches further than the two lower ones, so the flower is
   moved down (1.1 units) until its ink box, not its geometric centre, sits in the middle.

Two optical cuts share the construction:

| Cut   | Use         | Gap `g` | Core radius | Notch depth | Why                                                          |
| ----- | ----------- | ------- | ----------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| main  | > 32 px     | 4.2     | 7.4         | 6.2         | fine channels, calm notch                                    |
| small | 16 to 32 px | 6.6     | 9.2         | 7.6         | channels stay ≥ 1 px at 16 px; the notch and core still read |

In the site, `components/brand.tsx` renders the same geometry (`SakuraMark`, `Wordmark`) and
`components/seal.tsx` the seal: the mark on a blush disc with a rim inscription, which blooms in
(disc presses, petals open from the core in turn, rim settles) using transform and opacity only.

## Lockup

The mark is 1.62 × the wordmark's x-height, centred on the x-height band, followed by a gap of
0.34 × x-height. The word is lowercase `vow`, Funnel Display 600, tracked −0.02 em with the font's
kerning. In live UI the wordmark is text (`Wordmark`), so it inherits the theme.

## Clear space and minimum size

- Clear space: one core diameter (≈ 15% of the mark's height) on every side, mark or lockup.
- Minimum: 16 px for the small cut, 33 px for the main cut, 72 px wide for the lockup.

## Do

- Put the colour mark on paper, ink, blush or the hero clouds.
- Use the mono mark, in ink or paper, on photos, busy gradients and single-colour print.
- Keep the five petals upright: one petal points straight up.

## Don't

- Don't paint the gaps in a background colour, outline the mark or add strokes.
- Don't recolour the petals (other than mono), add gradients or shadows, or swap core and petals.
- Don't rotate, skew, squash or animate the mark outside the seal's bloom.
- Don't use sakura for small text on light backgrounds; use sakura ink.
- Don't scale the main cut below 33 px or the small cut above 32 px.
